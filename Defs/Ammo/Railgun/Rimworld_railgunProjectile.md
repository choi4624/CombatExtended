# Rimworld_railgunProjectile

> formula: P∝d×(L/d)^β(0.5)
> it is not perfectly matched

- High Diameter is important to damage target.
- Long length mostly important to penetrate target.
- over 1.5km/sec like railgun, hydrodynamic regime does not works. So `P≈L`. But High caliber works pentration in rimworld stat to play effieciently.
